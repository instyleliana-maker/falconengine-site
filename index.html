    Principle

    Data first. Hypothesis second.

    Every meaningful signal should be traceable to evidence. A confident answer without a source is not an answer.

    S
    Source
    Named and dated
    O
    Observation
    A measured fact, not a rewrite of one
    A
    Analysis
    Structured reasoning over the observation
    C
    Confidence
    Stated openly, sized to the evidence
    Design goals for every signal
    ✓
    Source Attributed
    Each signal is designed to name its original series or filing.
    ✓
    Timestamped
    Readings are designed to carry the time the source recorded them.
    ✓
    Traceable
    Derived figures are designed to be walked back to their inputs.
    ✓
    Confidence Aware
    Uncertainty is designed to be stated, not hidden.
